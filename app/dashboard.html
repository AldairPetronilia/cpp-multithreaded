Trading pipeline, live

Feed threads publish each instrument on its own topic. Every strategy runs on its own thread with its own bounded mailbox, subscribed only to its topics, and every order goes through one shared, rate-limited API gateway.

Try it: slow one strategy to 2 ms per event and watch only its mailbox fill and shed stale quotes while the others stay at zero; push the feed rate to max; raise broker latency or inject 429s and watch orders queue at the shared gateway instead of blocking any strategy.
