Trading pipeline, live

Feed threads publish each instrument on its own topic. Every strategy runs on its own thread with its own bounded mailbox, and every order goes through a pool of rate-limited API gateways. Every event is journaled, a supervisor replaces workers that crash or hang and rebuilds their state from the journal, and any recording can be replayed.

Try it: slow one strategy to 2 ms per event and watch only its mailbox fill and shed stale quotes while the others stay at zero; push the feed rate to max; raise broker latency or inject 429s and watch orders queue at the shared gateway instead of blocking any strategy.

Resilience: crash kills a worker thread, which rings the supervisor as it dies; hang makes its handler block, so the supervisor sees work waiting while nothing completes, abandons the thread, fences it and starts a replacement; poison makes every event throw. Replacements replay the journal silently (no stale orders) before they go live, and the execution worker asks the broker about every order it cannot account for before resending anything. Taking a gateway down moves its orders to the other one at once.

Latency percentiles come from fixed-size histograms (within ~3%). Data streams from this process over Server-Sent Events; nothing leaves your machine.
